**Gravesen context [T-TQWT]:** the mkIII TQWT uses two drivers and a stepped baffle;
the related DTQWT adds bass drivers and a deeper enclosure. The primary page
lists approximately 28 x 38 x 106 cm for TQWT and 28 x 51 x 106 cm for DTQWT,
with system sensitivity around 94 dB/2.8 V/1 m. These are designer specifications,
not measurements reproduced by this project. Do not mix original JA8008,
mkII, and HMQ/mkIII crossovers or cabinets.

## Meaning of proven

Prefer a published built system with repeatable plans, specified components,
electrical/acoustic evidence, and identified revisions. Community replication is
additional evidence; favorable listening descriptions alone are weaker.
King's design is proven by its author's documented build and tests, not by a
large independent sample of builders. His listening assessment is subjective.

Retain the published geometry before proposing improvements. A different driver,
larger opening, altered taper, new crossover, or uncalibrated stuffing is a new
variant requiring verification.
