# Selected transmission line

I chose **Martin J. King's Satori tapered TL** as our reference. It has an unusually
useful evidence trail: a built cabinet, measured driver parameters, dimensioned
plans, and comparisons between simulations and physical tests.

Its strengths are a manageable single fold, a substantial woofer, and a design
that can be reopened for damping experiments. The costs are premium drivers,
a fairly large cabinet, and careful sealing and stuffing work. It is a research
reference with limited independent replication, rather than the cheapest first
build. TriTrix is the economical alternative.

The ÆGIR reconstruction studies the enclosure and bass driver. It does not yet
validate the complete speaker, crossover, or stuffed response.

Source: [King's design report](http://quarter-wave.com/Project13/Satori_TL_Design_Documentation.pdf).
