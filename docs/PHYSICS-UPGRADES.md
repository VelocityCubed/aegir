# Loss, radiation, measured-response and cavity-mode upgrades

The0.9.4 [material models and wall lining](MATERIALS.md) extend the options below
with source-specific fibre/foam presets, Miki, JCA and published power-law models.
Existing historical and JCAL projects retain their numerical behaviour.

The current product interface is described in [STUDIO.md](STUDIO.md). Document
comparison panels are developer diagnostics, not the user workflow. Reference
samples no longer choose solver frequencies; rerun benchmarks for current values.

Status: implemented development features; complete Satori reproduction remains
unvalidated. All older projects retain their original physics unless the new
options are explicitly selected. Research data stays outside application packages.

Current circuit and 3D coupling additions, including the single-frequency material
contract, are documented in [CROSSOVER-BUILDER.md](CROSSOVER-BUILDER.md).

require a commercial solver. The desktop discovers a scientific runtime separately
from CadQuery. `AEGIR_PYTHON` can specify a compatible interpreter. The optional
runtime requirements are in `cad/requirements.txt`.

Reports: `test-results/satori-upgrades/comparison.html`, `comparison.json` and
`modal.json`. Baseline evidence is preserved in `test-results/satori/` and the
original example project. `examples/satori-upgraded.aegir.json` contains the new
Satori assumptions. It is generated by the upgrade benchmark, which never fits
or applies parameters to obtain agreement. No physical measurements were made.

## Propagation options

`system.physics` contains `ductLoss: none|thermoviscous|mjk-2003`,
`radiation: low-ka|piston`, and optional `stuffing[]` regions. Regions use normalized
closed-end-to-mouth `start/end` fractions, cannot overlap and split the acoustic
transfer matrix exactly at their boundaries. Each retains `source` and
`calibration: assumed|measured|reference-fit`. Legacy section attenuation remains
additional empirical Np/m; it is not silently reinterpreted as material density.

The equivalent-fluid transfer uses complex density and bulk modulus with exp(jωt):

```text
k = ω sqrt(rho_effective / K_effective)
gamma = j k + legacy_attenuation
Zc = sqrt(rho_effective K_effective) / area
A=D=cosh(gamma length); B=Zc sinh(gamma length); C=sinh(gamma length)/Zc
```

Branches select positive-real characteristic impedance and nonnegative attenuation
for the retained passive models. Non-finite transfer-matrix results fail explicitly
instead of producing plausible-looking charts.

### Geometry-based empty duct losses

The wide-duct model uses air viscosity from Sutherland's relation, Prandtl number
0.71 and section perimeter/area. Viscous and thermal penetration-depth corrections
modify density and compressibility. The approximation is rejected when the retained
viscous boundary-layer fraction exceeds0.25. It is not a resolved thermoviscous
solution at abrupt folds or submillimetre clearances.

Source: [COMSOL wide-duct/LRF descriptions](https://doc.comsol.com/6.4/doc/com.comsol.help.aco/aco_ug_pressure.05.186.html).

### Historical King2003 option

The source specifies an empty-tube flow resistance10Pa·s/m² and a density-dependent
fibre resistance1570D, where D is lb/ft³. The fibre contribution has the published
density-dependent high-pass order. The source defines `cycle=2*pi*rad` and
`Hz=cycle/sec` (DX3 worksheet p1). Thus `r*dω/(50Hz)` is `f/50` for the
application's frequency in ordinary Hz. Version0.9.4 incorrectly used `2*pi*f/50`;
the0.9.5 correction follows the explicit source unit definition, without fitting.
Effective density includes `-j lambda/omega`; the resulting propagation changes
both loss and phase. This is not an attenuation-only multiplier.

Sound speed is linearly interpolated between the source's retained calibration
points `(D,c)=(0,342),(.191,335),(.382,325),(.573,320)` and scaled relative to the
current air sound speed. **Interpolation and temperature scaling are our modelling
choices.** Density is restricted to this retained interval. The original formula
for the smooth sound-speed fit was not available as numerical input.

This empirical law was calibrated on King's earlier test tube and Hollofil. Its
use for the2023 Satori polyester filling is an explicit assumption; agreement with
one graph does not establish material equivalence. It is not a generic conversion
from arbitrary fibre density to acoustic properties.

Primary sources: [King2003 damping correlation](http://quarter-wave.com/TLs/Damping_Coefficient.pdf),
[wave-equation derivation](http://quarter-wave.com/TLs/Method_Derivation.pdf),
[calculation/radiation equations](http://quarter-wave.com/TLs/Calculation_Algorithm.pdf).

### JCAL materials

`material.model=jcal` requires flow resistivity Pa·s/m², porosity, tortuosity,
viscous/thermal characteristic lengths inµm and static thermal permeability m².
The implemented Johnson–Champoux–Allard–Lafarge equivalent fluid changes complex
inertia and compressibility. Bulk density alone cannot determine these parameters.
No Satori JCAL material is claimed: those transport measurements are absent.
Rigid-frame assumptions and the source's asymptotic limitations remain.

Source: [APMR JCAL equations and limitations](https://apmr.matelys.com/PropagationModels/MotionlessSkeleton/JohnsonChampouxAllardLafargeModel.html).
Tests check low/high-frequency limits and passive signs independently.

## Radiation and microphone coordinates

The full circular infinite-baffle load evaluates Bessel/Struve integral
representations. Independent SciPy special-function values check its resistance
and reactance from ka0.001 to30; the legacy low-ka expression is still selectable.
This removes a low-ka approximation, not the line model's transverse-mode limit.

`system.observation.positionMm` is an absolute cabinet-coordinate microphone
position. Each source's distance, propagation phase and piston directivity are
calculated separately, including the rear mouth's position. Mount
`acousticOffsetMm` moves the acoustic centre without changing its CAD hole.
The `space` option selects an ideal half-space or free-field coefficient.

`baffle=sphere-transition` adds the analytic transition
`H=(0.5+jka)/(1+jka)` with an explicitly supplied equivalent radius. This models a
low-to-high-frequency baffle-step transition; it **does not solve rectangular edge
diffraction or cabinet shadowing**. Satori uses half its width as an assumed radius,
not a fitted value. Keep this distinction visible in recommendations.

`operator=rayleigh` evaluates circular-piston near-field pressure in an infinite
half-space baffle. It uses the exact axial expression and Gauss/angular quadrature
off axis. It requires a probe at least0.1mm in front of the source plane. The
rectangular port is still an equivalent disk, requiring additional validation for
near-field port comparisons. Probe locations/voltages missing from a reference
still block verification. Sphere transition and free-field scaling cannot be mixed
with this infinite-baffle near-field operator.

Source/analytic validation: [COMSOL baffled-piston Rayleigh benchmark](https://doc.comsol.com/6.4/doc/com.comsol.help.models.aco.baffled_piston_radiation/baffled_piston_radiation.html).

## Measured-data crossover path

Each mount can import FRD/ZMA/CSV response data with Hz,magnitude,phase(degrees).
Two-column data is retained with null phase; zero phase is never invented. Imports
reject invalid/unsorted data and never extrapolate. Metadata is editable in the
system-study JSON. It must establish voltage, distance, microphone coordinates,
common timing, final-baffle inclusion and driven terminus coverage.

`analysis.measured` uses the measured mounted impedance as each passive circuit's
actual load and the measured acoustic group per terminal voltage. It does not add
the simulated line output a second time. Cabinet-loaded groups must include their
terminus contribution. Multiple cabinet-loaded sources require mutual/multiport
impedance information and are rejected by this independent-branch path.

Common microphone position and timing must be confirmed. Repositioning a measured
woofer-plus-port group is not inferred from one acoustic centre. Active/differently
excited branches have no aggregate amplifier impedance reported. The path does not
infer excursion, port velocity or headroom from acoustic SPL; no fake zero-valued
headroom is displayed. Synthetic imports test the circuitry; no real Satori mounted
complex response files have been supplied.

## Cavity modes and numerical resolution

`analysis.modal` uses the actual cabinet solids, not `system.profile`. It constructs
a symmetric finite-volume rigid-cavity operator with sealed driver apertures and
pressure zero only on the physical mouth plane, including the stock-depth neck.
The optional SciPy backend solves the lowest3D modes and returns eigenpair residuals,
air volume, resolved opening area and mesh-to-mesh changes. It includes transverse
modes absent from a1D line. Cancellation terminates the solver process tree on Windows.

An independent analytic tube test verifies second-order refinement. A rectangular
cavity test verifies transverse modes and the discrete spectrum. The deterministic
random start vector avoids suppressing antisymmetric modes. Disconnected domains
are rejected instead of silently removing unwanted modes.

The geometry is staircase-volume, with exact solid-crossing face barriers. This
is not a conforming/cut-cell FEM. Even a small eigenpair residual does not imply
small geometry error. `meshStableWithin1Percent` requires both modal and volume
changes below1% between the last two meshes; it is a stability check, not physical
validation. Mode order may exchange near crossings, so shapes should eventually
be retained for matching. Current budget:180000cells and120seconds per solve.

Satori refinement is **not yet stable**: first modes at15,10,8mm are about
34.215,35.244,34.341Hz. Corresponding volumes are72.565,73.626,72.251L. The10mm
frequency happens to be close to published35.4Hz; selecting it for that reason
would be invalid. The underspecified divider end seals and a conforming geometry
discretization remain important next work.

## Reference improvements and current numbers

`scripts/extract-satori-impedance.py` extracts approximate calculated solid curves
from the PDF's embedded JPEGs. The continuous second peak is separated from the
shifted dashed measured peak using contiguous stroke runs. Retained overlays were
visually inspected. Raster ambiguity remains:1Ω amplitude uncertainty,2% frequency
uncertainty. The dense samples are correlated, not independent measurements.
Measured dashed data is not silently used as calculated data.

On the same extracted Figure15 grid (~10–990Hz), with no fitted parameters:

| Model | RMS impedance error | First peak |
|---|---|---|
| Legacy lossless | 3.202Ω | 49.66Ω at21.23Hz |
| Wide-duct losses/full piston | 2.848Ω | 45.70Ω at21.13Hz |
| Historical King2003 tube/full piston | 2.277Ω | 30.43Ω at21.23Hz |
| Published solid raster | — | 29.84Ω at20.63Hz |

The first height improves strongly; frequency remains about2.94% high. The second
model peak is43.84Ω at49.70Hz versus extracted40.99Ω at48.24Hz. Peak widths and
unmatched peaks remain in the evidence; they are not hidden by a total RMS score.
The earlier5.68Ω result used a different sparse10–45Hz target and must not be
compared directly with these full-grid RMS values.

The assumed partial King2003 filling gives about0.822Ω RMS discrepancy against
the Figure16 solid raster. Its weak lower peak is unmatched and the upper peak's
height/width still differ. The full passive SPL discrepancy falls from7.831 to
about2.204dB on the same sparse Figure18 points after adding filling, source paths
and the sphere baffle-step approximation. **This remains diagnostic:** reference
voltage is unknown, the material transfer is assumed, exact diffraction is absent
and the1D line is used beyond its transverse-mode range.

`analysis.sensitivity` performs bounded independent perturbations and records
their RMS/peak changes without fitting, selecting or applying a candidate.
Calibration on a reference curve and validation against independent measurements
are distinct milestones. `npm run verify:satori` continues to fail complete
speaker acceptance; the original baseline is deliberately preserved.
