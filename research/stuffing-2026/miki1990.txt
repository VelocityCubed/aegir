The characteristic impedance and propagation constant of porous materials are dis-
cussed from a viewpoint of physical realizability, and new regression models based on
experimental data by Delany and Bazley are derived. Modifications from their original
models are so made that the impedance function satisfies the positive-real property and
that the propagation constant written in terms of complex variables becomes a regular
function in the right half-plane. The new models are shown to be useful for the predic-
tion of acoustical behavior of porous materials, especially in the double-layer case,
even outside the frequency range of validity of the original models.
